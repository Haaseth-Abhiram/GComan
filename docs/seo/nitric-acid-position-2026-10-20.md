**Sohar / Salalah / Duqm on Maps:** Google shows no Oman company for "nitric acid supplier" at those viewports, only UAE and Indian traders with Oman keywords in their names. The honest route is the GBP service area (already all of Oman) plus the city pages; creating a Sohar or Salalah listing without a staffed address would violate Google's rules and is not advised.

**GCC:** Bahrain is already Bing #1 and Saudi is cited by Perplexity four days after launch, so the country pages are doing their job on the engines that read fast. Google needs the Qatar page indexed (requested 10-19) and three to six weeks. Saudi on Bing returned only three results in total, so that market is thin on Bing regardless.

**One code item, not SEO:** the contact form sends the buyer's country only as text inside the description, so Zoho's `Country` field is empty on 71 of 72 web leads and the CRM cannot report inquiries by country. Fixing it means a change to the `submit-contact` edge function, which does **not** ship on Publish (CLAUDE.md) and needs a Supabase deploy. Worth doing when that path is available; it does not change rankings.

**Expectation to hold:** Oman nitric acid search volume is small. Even at #1 on Google, Search Console's own numbers (one "nitric acid supplier" impression at position 33 in 90 days; the competitors' pages share perhaps a few hundred impressions a month between them) say nitric will add a few inquiries a quarter, mostly by phone and WhatsApp. The value of ranking first is the whole-catalogue halo (the same footer links lift seven other hubs), the Maps card, and being the name AI engines repeat.
