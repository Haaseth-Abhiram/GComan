# Nitric acid — market position & strategy, 2026-10-05

Written because Global Chemicals now **manufactures** nitric acid, which changes what we can credibly claim and what price we can compete at. Measured, not estimated: all figures from Search Console (classic + generative AI) and live localized SERPs.

---

## 1. The one-line diagnosis

**We rank well for a market that barely exists, and are invisible in the market we actually serve.**

Our two nitric acid pages are *Food Grade* and *Laboratory Grade*. Every competitor on page 1 sells **industrial/technical grade in bulk**. We have no industrial page at all — so we don't compete for the commercial demand, we just don't appear.

### The pattern across both SERPs

Winners consistently publish, in the title and snippet:
1. **Grade** — industrial / technical, not food or lab
2. **Concentration** — 68%, 58–62%, 68–72%

5. **"Manufacturer"** — claimed loosely by traders
6. **Price** — rarely, and it earns a rich result when present

We publish none of these six for nitric acid.

## 5. Why this is now winnable

**Because we actually manufacture it, and they mostly don't.** Muscat Chemical and rx-sol call themselves "manufacturer" while trading; a genuine producer can state capacity, batch QC, CoA on every lot and a real lead time — claims a reseller cannot match and that both Google and AI engines reward as specificity.

Three further advantages already in hand:
- **JSRS registration + ICV + ISO 9001** — procurement-grade credentials none of the page-1 Oman competitors lead with. This is exactly the pattern that made the JSRS blog the site's single most-cited page.
- **AI already surfaces us in Oman/Saudi/UAE** for this product. We are strengthening a channel that is already working, not starting cold.
- **Price intent is unanswered.** A published, honest price band (or a clear "indicative OMR/MT, volume-tiered") would differentiate immediately against eight competitors who hide it.

## 6. Recommended plan

### Phase 1 — the missing page (the whole gap, in one asset)

**Create `/products/acids/nitric-acid-industrial` (or `/chemical-supplier/nitric-acid-oman` as a product+geo hub).**

This is **additive and carries no risk to existing rankings** — a brand-new URL with nothing to lose, unlike a retitle. It is the single highest-value action available for this product.

It must carry, because this is what the SERP rewards:

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| H1 / title | `Nitric Acid 68% Supplier & Manufacturer in Oman` |
| Opening 40 words | Definitional: *"Nitric acid (HNO₃, CAS 7697-37-2) is a strong mineral acid supplied at 68% concentration for metal passivation, pickling…"* — AI engines lift this |
| Grades table | concentration × grade × application × pack |
| Pack formats | cans / drums / IBC / tanker — with weights |
| Applications | metal pickling & passivation, stainless-steel treatment, water treatment, fertilizer intermediate, electronics etch, dairy/CIP |
| Cities | Muscat · Sohar · Salalah · Duqm · Rusayl — plus Saudi / UAE / Bahrain export |
| Credentials | JSRS · ICV · ISO 9001 · SDS + CoA per lot |
| Price | an indicative band or "volume-tiered, request quote" — answer the PAA question |
| FAQs | lift the four "People also ask" questions verbatim |
| Schema | Product + FAQPage + Breadcrumb, visible FAQ via `FAQSection` |

**I need real data from you before writing it** — I will not invent manufacturing claims:
1. Concentrations produced (68%? 58–62%? both?)
2. Pack formats and weights you can actually ship
3. Capacity / lead time, and whether tanker or IBC is available
4. Any price band you are willing to publish
5. Is the plant JSRS/ICV-covered for this product?

### Phase 2 — fix the two existing pages

- `/products/food-beverage/nitric-acid-68` sits in a **food** category, which caps its reach. Keep it (food-grade CIP is a real niche we rank 4.9 for) but **link it to the new industrial hub** and let the hub own the generic terms.
- Lab-grade page: thin at 1,011 chars with only 2 FAQs. Deepen or point it at the hub.

### Phase 3 — the GCC gaps

- **Bahrain is zero in both channels.** Nothing to defend, nothing to lose — add it to the cities list rather than building a page for it yet.
- **UAE converts at 3.7% against Oman's 9.0%** site-wide. Dubi Chem and Chemway sell into Muscat; a nitric-acid hub naming both markets defends and attacks at once.

### Timing

Phase 1 is a **new page**, so it does not touch the measurement window's protected assets and cannot cost existing rankings. It is the one piece of conditional work I would start before 27 Oct — the business reason (new manufacturing capacity) is real and the technical risk is close to zero.

Phases 2–3 involve editing pages that already rank and should wait for the watch to close.
