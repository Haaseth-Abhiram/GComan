## 7. Advice (ranked by expected leads per hour of work)

1. **Win the clicks we already rank for.** We are top 3 for "chemical supplier in oman / muscat" (570 impressions in 3 months) and get 4 clicks, because the Maps pack and AI Overview sit above us. The lever is the Maps pack: reviews (we have 2, the #1 has 10–19), GBP photos and posts, WhatsApp on GBP. Second lever: the homepage title and snippet, which is on the HANDOFF hold list and needs the user's decision.
2. **Lubricants are the biggest product theme** in search (116 queries), in AI (374 + 261 + 95 impressions) and in leads (21). Brand searches ("exxonmobil oman" 3.9, "castrol oman" 8.2) are close. Lever: confirm the Shell/Mobil/Castrol supply status (owner fact, open), then give the brand pages the confirmed grades and pack sizes.
3. **Urea / fertilizer**: four quote forms, Arabic "Omani urea" at position 6. Arabic batch 7 should take the urea and gypsum Arabic search terms.
4. **Arabic pool chemicals** sit at 11–11.6 for 68 impressions: the nearest Arabic page-1 move. Check that the /ar pool hub carries the exact phrase "كيماويات حمامات السباحة" in its title and H1.
5. **Acetic acid supplier (124 impressions at 44)**, glycols (21–32) and corrosion inhibitors (35–52): real demand, ranked low. The cap is links (`nitric-authority-gap`), not copy. No new pages; these go in outreach and directory product lists.
6. **Competitor-name searches** ("muscat chemical" 222, "gulf international chemicals" 57): do not target another company's name. They show RXSOL's brand pull, which comes from 41 referring domains against our handful.
7. **GCC country keywords are tiny** (UAE 113, Saudi 64 impressions in 3 months). No new country pages; win them through ensun.io and yellowpages-uae listings (peer map).
8. **Lead hygiene**: record the country on every CRM lead and tag vendor pitches, so the next review can split demand by market.
9. **Bing data**: the Bing Webmaster account in this Chrome no longer has access to gcoman.com ("User is unauthorized"). The user should check User management in Bing Webmaster, or tell Claude which Chrome is signed in to the owning account. Then Bing Search Performance and AI Performance grounding queries (Copilot's actual questions) join this map.
