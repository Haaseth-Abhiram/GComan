## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Keyword Stuffing:** Don't repeat "chemical supplier Oman" 50 times in description
❌ **Fake Reviews:** Never pay for reviews or create fake ones (Google will penalize)
❌ **Inconsistent NAP:** Ensure Name, Address, Phone are identical everywhere (website, GBP, directories)
❌ **Ignoring Reviews:** Always respond to reviews (shows you care)
❌ **No Photos:** GBP with photos get 42% more direction requests
❌ **Outdated Hours:** Update for holidays, or customers will complain
❌ **No Posts:** Inactive profiles rank lower
