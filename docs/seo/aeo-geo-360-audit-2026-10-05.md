## 3. Product-level visibility

**333 product URLs** draw impressions (including `/ar` twins); together they take **104 clicks / 3.29K impressions**, position 10.4.

| Page | Clicks | Impressions | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/products/lubricants` | 25 | 425 | 5.9% |
| `/products/lubricants/mobil-lubricants` | 7 | 88 | 8.0% |
| **`/products/food-beverage/nitric-acid-68`** | **5** | **162** | **3.1%** |
| `/products/agro/urea-fertilizer` | 5 | 75 | 6.7% |
| `/products/lubricants/shell-lubricants` | 5 | 17 | 29.4% |
| **`/ar/products/agro/agricultural-gypsum`** | **3** | **111** | 2.7% |
| `/products/swimming-pool` | 3 | 71 | 4.2% |

Three observations:

1. **Lubricants is 36% of all product clicks** (37 of 104). The catalogue is one category deep commercially.
2. **Arabic product pages genuinely perform** — three of the top ten are `/ar`. The Arabic investment is paying off, which the earlier audits never confirmed with click data.
3. **The tail is flat.** 333 URLs, 104 clicks — the median product page earns zero. More pages is not the answer; depth on the few that rank is.

**Nitric acid is the clearest single-product opportunity:** 162 impressions, 102 AI impressions, but **3.1% CTR**. High visibility, poor click capture — a title/snippet problem, not a ranking problem.

## 4. A regression I introduced — Arabic pages show English FAQs

Lovable's issue scanner flagged it and it is substantially right.

**What I did:** added `<FAQSection>` to the homepage, About, Contact, Customers, Certifications and Industries, with a guard meant to hide it on `/ar` until Arabic copy exists.

**Why the guard fails:** it tests `i18n.language === 'ar'`, but during prerender those pages render while the language is still `en`, so the English branch is taken and English FAQ text is baked into the `/ar` HTML.

**Verified on the live site:** `/ar/` serves `<html lang="en">`, an English `<title>`, an English `<h1>`, `og:locale: en_US`, **and `<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>` with English Q&A**.

**Severity — lower than it first appears, and I want to be precise:**
- `/ar/` canonicalises to `https://gcoman.com/` (the English homepage). Google consolidates it; there is **no duplicate-content or ranking risk**.
- Those `/ar` pages were *already* prerendering as English duplicates before my change — my addition made it more visible, it did not create the underlying behaviour.
- `/ar` **product** pages are unaffected and correct: Arabic title, Arabic `h1`, self-referencing canonical. The pages that are actually indexed are fine.

**So: a real UX defect on six non-indexed pages, not an SEO incident.** An Arabic visitor landing on `/ar/` sees English — which they already did for the rest of the page.

**Fix:** derive the language from the URL path rather than `i18n.language`, so the gate holds at prerender time. Small and additive. **Not shipping it mid-watch without your say-so** — it is a UX fix, not an emergency.

Lovable's second issue — lazy-loaded auth can hang on an endless spinner if the chunk fetch fails — affects **admin users only**, not visitors or SEO. Worth fixing, no urgency.
