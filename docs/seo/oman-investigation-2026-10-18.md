## 1. "chemical suppliers oman" #2 vs "chemical suppliers in oman" #1: what's going on

**We did not fall.** The "#1" for "chemical suppliers oman" (2026-10-11 morning) was a **signed-in, personalised** reading. The unpersonalised check that afternoon, and GSC's 28-day average (2.0 to 2.3), already put us at #2 (visibility ledger, 2026-10-11 pm correction). Same-method readings: #2 on 10-11, #2 on 10-18.

**Why the word "in" swaps #1 and #2.** Both queries return the same 8 sites:

| # | "chemical suppliers **in** oman" | "chemical suppliers oman" |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **gcoman.com**: "Global Chemicals LLC: Chemical Suppliers in Oman & GCC" | omanchem.com: "Chemical Supplier & Company in Muscat, Oman" |
| 2 | omanchem.com | **gcoman.com** |
| 3 | yellowpages.om | yellowpages.om |
| 4 | soharchemical.com | soharchemical.com |
| 5–8 | muscatchemical, omanchem, lookchem, **gcoman.com (blog guide)** | omanchem, muscatchemical, lookchem, ooiss |

- With "in", the query matches our homepage title **word for word** ("Chemical Suppliers in Oman"). We win, and we also get a second listing (the 2026 suppliers guide).
- Without "in", the closest match is the **domain name itself**. "omanchem" reads as "oman chem(ical)", and its title also ends "…in Muscat, Oman". Google breaks the near-tie in its favour.
- **How much it matters:** in 90 days of Oman data, GSC shows **no impressions for "chemical suppliers oman"**, and **45 impressions / 9 clicks at average position 2.2 for "chemical suppliers in oman"**. Oman searchers type the "in" version, which is the one we lead. The swap is real, but it decides only a handful of searches a quarter.
- omanchem.com, muscatchemical.com and soharchemical.com share one phone number (+968 9948 9269): **one operator, three exact-match domains**. This network is the competitor on almost every Oman query below.

## 6. Why (root causes, in order of weight)

1. **Few referring domains.** About 5 domains, 1 on Bing (tamol.om). The network has three exact-match domains plus hundreds of thin pages interlinked. This is the ceiling on every product and city query (established 2026-09-08 and unchanged).
2. **Maps: 2 reviews** against 3 to 22 for the listings around us. Maps decides "near me" (166 impressions) and every city search.
3. **Directory pages on page 1 that we're not on**: yellowpages.om (#3 on both head terms), lookchem (#7), ensun, cybo, madeinomangate. Being listed puts us on page 1 a second time, through pages Google already trusts.
4. **City pages lose to the homepage** (Muscat, Salalah, Duqm), and Rusayl and Sohar have no Google position at all. The city pages are indexed but have no external signal pointing at them.
5. **No presence for Nizwa, Sur, Barka, Ibri, Seeb or Buraimi.** But GSC shows **zero** demand there in 90 days. muscatchemical wins with thin taxonomy pages because nobody else competes.
6. **The hubs were never indexed** until tonight (Soft 404 bug, fixed in `ab70162`; 6 indexing requests accepted on 10-18).
