# GC Oman Product Positioning Gap Audit — 2026-09-21

**Every canonical product (274; 3 caustic twins canonicalised) positioned against real Google data.** Source: full GSC page dataset (28d, Aug 24–Sep 20) + 35 Oman-localized live SERP checks (this week) + competitor vertical mapping. Method notes at the end.

## The five positioning bands

| Band | Meaning | Count | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — WINNING** | pos ≤10 with real demand (≥10 impr) | **33** | Working: protect & push to #1 |
| **B — RANKS-UNSEEN** | pos ≤10 but <10 impressions | **127** | The core GC gap: Google ranks us well *on micro-tail queries only* — the head term for these products is owned by competitors, so almost nobody ever sees us |
| **C — NEAR-MISS** | pos 10–20 | **33** | One push from page 1: snippets + internal links + depth |
| **D — WEAK** | pos 20–50 | **12** | Wrong battlefield or authority-bound |
| **E — BURIED** | pos 50+ | **6** | Global commodity terms; retarget or ignore |
| **Z — INVISIBLE** | zero impressions in 28 days | **63** | Google shows us for nothing: either no search demand for the product name (house brands) or no crawl/index traction |

**The headline finding:** the gap is NOT ranking — 160 of 274 products already average position ≤10. The gap is **demand capture**: only 33 products get meaningful impressions. Competitors don't outrank us on the tail — they own the *head terms* ("X supplier oman") through domain authority and 60k-page footprints, so the searches happen on their results, not ours. Leads come from head-term presence + local pack + JSRS/vendor-list credibility — exactly where the improvement plan below aims.

## The 63 invisible products — root causes

Grouped by why Google shows them for nothing:
- **House-brand names with zero search demand** (most tank-cleaners: Aqua Clean HD variants, Edge Sol, Universal Wash; cooling-water Altreat/Corro-Guard; additives): nobody types these names. Fix = retitle/retarget each page to its *application* term ("ship hold cleaner Oman", "closed-loop biocide"), keep the brand as secondary.
- **Oilfield specialty SKUs (20)**: real demand exists but sits inside PDO/EPC procurement, not public search. Fix = JSRS/vendor-qualification content per family (the proven #1 pattern) + application terms (completion brine, H2S scavenger class).
- **Crawl-recovery stragglers**: some were only reachable after the Sep-10 category-link fix; indexed count is rising (371→449). Re-check this band monthly before spending content effort.

## The positioning strategy that produces leads (priority order)

1. **Head-term presence where identity wins** — supplier+product+Oman pages with exact-phrase titles (done catalog-wide) can only take head terms where authority is contestable: agro, lab, glycols, corrosion inhibitors, food-grade. Push those 5 verticals hard; do not bleed effort into MBBT/RXSOL strongholds until backlinks land.
2. **Local pack** — we already appear for calcium chloride & industrial salt with 2 reviews. Reviews (→25+) + GBP product list = pack presence across dozens of product queries, above organic. Fastest lead lever that exists.
3. **Vendor-list credibility** — JSRS/PDO/Duqm content generates #1s and matches how Oman oil&gas actually buys. One page per major family (oilfield, water-treatment, lab).
4. **B-band conversion** — the 127 ranks-unseen products need *query surface*, not rank: add grade/pack/application variants to titles+FAQs (FAQ text now crawler-visible after the forceMount fix) and Arabic twins (already indexable) to multiply the tail until authority unlocks heads. **FAQs DONE 2026-09-26** — 91 of 127 had zero FAQs at all; added 211 new FAQ entries (grade+packaging question, applications question) across 116 of the 127, generated from each product's own already-published `technicalSpecs`/`applications` fields (no invented facts) — the other 11 already had equivalent coverage and were left alone. Titles were deliberately left untouched (these products already earn their position ≤10 on some query; retitling risks losing that for an unproven gain). Arabic twins already exist for all 275/275 products per the site-wide translation project — nothing further needed there. Verified: FAQ text appears twice in every touched page's prerendered HTML (FAQPage JSON-LD + visible accordion DOM, matching the C1 forceMount fix), tsc/lint/sitemap-health/ar-meta/prerender-titles/internal-links-audit/77 e2e all clean. First movement to check ~3-4 weeks out (GSC needs a few weeks to show impression-count shifts on this band).
5. **C-band sprint** — 33 near-misses: hand-written snippets + 2–3 internal links each; measure in 3 weeks. **DONE 2026-09-26** — snippets shipped 09-23/09-26 (dd6a50c, 5e2f873); internal links shipped 09-26 via a new `relatedProducts` pill-link block on `BlogPost.tsx` (same idiom as the existing `relatedLocations` block), populated on the 12 topically-relevant blog posts (`lab-chemical-safety`, `jsrs-chemical-supplier-pdo-oman`, `oil-gas-chemicals-trends`, `cooling-tower-maintenance`, `water-treatment-trends-2025`, `meg-vs-deg-gas-dehydration-oman-gcc`, `swimming-pool-chemicals-oman-muscat`, `shell-mobil-castrol-lubricants-oman`, `industrial-lubricants-guide`, `agricultural-chemicals-oman-buyers-guide`, `food-grade-chemicals-supplier-oman`, `top-chemical-suppliers-oman-2026`) — every one of the 33 got 1 hand-placed, on-topic blog link (`oxygen-scavenger` got 2), on top of the category-listing + automatic same-category rotation links each product already had.
