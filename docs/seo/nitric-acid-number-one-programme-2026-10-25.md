# Nitric acid: why we are not #1 on Google, and the programme to get there (2026-10-25)

Asked by the user on 2026-10-25: why gcoman.com is not #1 on Google for nitric acid, why it does not appear for "nitric acid supplier" anywhere in the GCC, and a systematic plan covering every engine and every country.

Builds on [`nitric-acid-competitors-2026-10-19.md`](./nitric-acid-competitors-2026-10-19.md) (items N1–N11), [`nitric-acid-keywords-2026-10-22.md`](./nitric-acid-keywords-2026-10-22.md) and today's [`nitric-acid-gcc-position-2026-10-25.md`](./nitric-acid-gcc-position-2026-10-25.md). Four sources, labelled and never blended: Search Console (owner account), Bing Webmaster Tools, the live engines in the signed-in Chrome, and the live site. Google's search page could not be read today because it showed a CAPTCHA after 5 queries, so the Google rank cells are the last reading (10-19 / 10-22).

## 1. The answer in one paragraph

Google indexes all six nitric pages, and they are the most complete pages in the market. Google does not rank them because **other websites do not point to gcoman.com**. Google counts links from about 6 other websites to the whole site, and none to a nitric or product page. Bing counts 1. The Oman #1 has 41, most of them from its own family of sister sites. The pages are also young: the hub is 20 days old and the country pages 9 days. Outside Oman there is a second wall. Google, Maps and the AI engines prefer a supplier with a local address, so an Oman exporter ranks for "from Oman / GCC / bulk" wording before it ranks for "supplier in Kuwait". More pages or more words will not fix either problem. Links, listings, reviews and time will.

Maps will never show us in Dubai or Riyadh without a staffed address there, and inventing one is against Google's rules.

## 5. Will not do

No sister-site network, no bought or swapped links, no bulk directory submissions of the chemstock kind, no nitric × city pages, no fake local addresses, no retitling the 277 products, no more FAQs for the sake of FAQs. Each would either break Google's spam policies or repeat work that measurably did not move rank.
