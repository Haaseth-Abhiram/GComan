## 4. Why

1. **No Saudi entity.** Every page-1 organic result on both engines is a `.sa` / Saudi-registered company. "supplier in <city>" carries local intent and Google fills it with in-country businesses; it shows our city pages to Saudi users only for "near me" queries, at positions 5–9, where they don't click. The city pages are indexed and healthy; they are simply the wrong nationality for the query.
2. **Directories hold the only open page-1 slots**: ensun.io (#2–3 Google, **#1 Bing**, for the country head terms, Qatar and Kuwait too), lookchem (#8–9 Google, #10 Bing), atninfo (#3 Riyadh on Google), saudiayp.com (Bing #4 Dammam, #8 Riyadh), aeroleads / scribd lists. We are on none of them.
3. **Referring domains** (about 5), the same ceiling as Oman. It decides the product long tail ("industrial surfactants" at 39).
4. **The frame we win has no demand.** Saudi buyers search for a local supplier, not an Omani exporter. The export guide's #1 is real and worth keeping; it just isn't where the searches are.
