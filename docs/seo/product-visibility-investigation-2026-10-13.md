# Why gcoman.com wins "chemical suppliers in Oman" and loses every product — investigation of 2026-10-13

Scope: SEO, AEO (answer engines) and GEO (generative engines), every product line, read live on 2026-10-13 from the signed-in Mac Chrome (owner account `globalchemicalsllc8@gmail.com`). Each number is labelled: *per Search Console* (GSC), *per Google* (fetched SERP, `gl=om`, `pws=0`), *per Bing* (rendered, `cc=OM`), *per AI Mode / ChatGPT / Perplexity* (rendered; AI Mode and Perplexity are signed in and therefore personalised), *per Zoho CRM*, *per the live site*. Bing Webmaster Tools and Gemini/Copilot were not read: `n/m`.

## 1. The answer

1. **People do not find us by product. They find us by name.** *Per GSC*, of 525 clicks in 90 days, **450 (86 %) are brand searches** — ours and the look-alike "Global Chemicals & Maintenance Systems". Product searches are **624 of the 1,000 recorded queries but 16 clicks**.
2. **For the commodity products we sell most, Google showed us zero times** in 90 days for any "product + Oman" query: caustic soda 0, sodium hypochlorite 0, hydrochloric acid 0, sulphuric acid 0, PAC 0, citric acid 0, calcium chloride 0, methanol 0, hydrogen peroxide 0. *Per Google*, across **36 product searches localised to Oman we are in the top 8 for none**; omanchem.com is in the top 4 for 30 of them.
3. **It is not indexing and not page quality.** *Per GSC*, the English product pages are indexed (the 133 "discovered, not indexed" URLs are all Arabic twins). Our caustic soda page has 1,330 words and 89 internal links; omanchem's has 487. The three product+geo hubs, our deepest pages, were Soft 404 until today's Request indexing.
4. **They are first on authority and footprint, and we help them by splitting our own products.** omanchem.com ≈ **35,400** indexed pages and 41 referring domains against our ≈ 487 and 1; a brand name that *is* the search term; a page-1 directory slot in every market we are absent from; and on our side, **33 chemicals spread across 86 grade-variant pages** so no single URL can carry "sodium hypochlorite supplier oman".
5. **The AI engines already rank us better than Google does.** *Per AI Mode and ChatGPT*, Global Chemicals LLC is the first-named distributor for caustic soda / hypochlorite in Oman, behind the manufacturer Oman Chlorine. *Per Bing*, the hubs are #1 (HCl), #3 (caustic soda, hypochlorite), #5 (PAC). The Google gap is the outlier, and it has a specific shape.
