## 3. Why Oman Chemical is first, mechanically

Their sitemap, read today: **14,065 URLs — 7,018 product pages (≈4,600 distinct product stems, RXSOL catalogue plus lab reagents) and 6,954 content pages, of which 6,874 are "<Product> Supplier in Sohar Muscat Barka Ruwi Madayn" city pages.** Google indexes ≈35,400 of their URLs; muscatchemical runs the same template at ≈28,900. One thin page per product × Oman, and Google treats the domain as the topical authority for "any chemical + Oman" — which is why they also rank on bare global terms ("isopropyl alcohol", "calcium chloride", "hydrogen peroxide") from an Oman IP.

What that machine cannot do, and we can: a specification table, pack range and FAQ per product (they have none); a hub that reads as the canonical answer; AI engines that already name us first among distributors.

What they have that we lack, in order: 41 referring domains (we have 1); 35,000 indexed URLs (we have 487); a brand name that is the query; listings on every directory that itself ranks; 156 reviews on one page-1 competitor (we have 2). And on our side: 33 chemicals split across 86 pages (first four fixed today), hubs that were Soft 404, 45 product pages under 200 words, a median product page of 292 words, 78 products without FAQs.
