# Earned-link outreach: the four emails for Workstream A8 (2026-10-25)

Drafted by Claude for [`nitric-acid-number-one-programme-2026-10-25.md`](./nitric-acid-number-one-programme-2026-10-25.md) Workstream A, item **A8** ("Earned links: OCCI member directory, customers' and principals' supplier pages, certification body client list. U (+ C drafts the emails)"). The kit [`authority-building-kit-2026-09-26.md`](./authority-building-kit-2026-09-26.md) §3 already holds GPCA, Ithraa and MEDRC; these four were not drafted anywhere.

**Why these four and not more directories.** Google's Links report counts about 6 sites linking to gcoman.com, Bing counts 1; the Oman #1 has 41, mostly a sister-site group we will not copy. A link from a chamber, a customer's approved-supplier page, a principal's "where to buy" page or a certifier's client list is the kind nobody in this market has, and it is weighed more than a directory entry. Target from the programme: referring domains 1 → 15 by 2026-12-31, A8 contributing 2–3 by January.

**Not in this list, on purpose.** The Tender Board (Decision 104/2022 registry) and JSRS publish no supplier list (checked 2026-10-20, MASTER-PLAN P5.10), so neither can be a referring domain. Registration there is a sales matter, not a link.

## 1. OCCI — Oman Chamber of Commerce and Industry (member directory listing)

Send from the company Gmail to the OCCI membership desk (occi.om → Membership / Contact). If Global Chemicals LLC is already an OCCI member (CR renewal requires it), ask only for the directory listing and the website field.

> **Subject:** Member directory listing — Global Chemicals LLC (CR [number])
>
> Dear OCCI Membership team,
>
> Global Chemicals LLC (CR [number]) is an OCCI member and an Omani chemical supplier based in Ghala, Muscat. We manufacture and supply industrial, water-treatment, oilfield, laboratory and food-contact chemicals, including nitric acid 68% from our own production unit, and we export to the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.
>
> Could you confirm our entry in the OCCI member directory shows the following, and add the website if the field is empty?
>
> Business name: Global Chemicals LLC
> Address: Office-3, Building 1737, Near Oman LNG, Ghala, Muscat, Oman (PC 111)
> Phone: +968 9277 2305
> Email: info@gcoman.com
> Website: https://gcoman.com
> Activity: Chemical supplier / wholesaler (ISO 9001:2015, JSRS registered, ICV compliant)
>
> If the directory entry is self-managed through the member portal, a pointer to the right page is enough and we will update it ourselves.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Name, title]
> Global Chemicals LLC | gcoman.com | +968 9277 2305

**Done when:** the OCCI directory page for Global Chemicals LLC is live with `https://gcoman.com` as a link (open it in a browser, not from the sender's memory). Then Claude adds that URL to Organization `sameAs` in `index.html` (MASTER-PLAN P5.9).

## 2. A customer's approved-supplier / vendor page (one email per customer)

Send to the procurement or supplier-relations contact at a customer that publishes a supplier list (large Oman industrials, utilities, EPC contractors and some free-zone operators do; check the customer's website for "Suppliers", "Vendors", "Partners" or "Approved vendors" before writing). Start with the customers who buy nitric acid or other hub products, because the KPI is a link to a product page.

> **Subject:** Listing Global Chemicals LLC on your approved-supplier page
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> Thank you for the continued business with Global Chemicals LLC. We supply [product(s) and pack, e.g. nitric acid 68% in 35 kg carboys] to [site / plant] under [PO or contract reference, if useful].
>
> We noticed [Company] publishes its approved suppliers at [URL of their supplier page]. Could Global Chemicals LLC be added there? The details that match our other registrations are:
>
> Global Chemicals LLC — chemical supplier, Muscat, Oman
> Website: https://gcoman.com
> Product page, if your list links to products: https://gcoman.com/chemical-supplier/nitric-acid-oman

>
> If the page is maintained by another team, I would be grateful for the right contact.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Name, title]
> Global Chemicals LLC | gcoman.com | +968 9277 2305

**Done when:** the customer's page lists us with a link. Record the URL in the ledger; one customer link to the nitric hub counts toward the "≥5 referring domains to a nitric page" target.

## 3. A principal's / manufacturer's "distributors" or "where to buy" page (one email per principal)

For every manufacturer whose products we distribute under an agreement (the principals named on the product pages and in the Zoho vendor list). Most manufacturers keep a "Distributors by country" or "Where to buy" page and add a reseller on request, because it brings them enquiries.

## 4. The ISO 9001:2015 certification body (client list / certified-company register)

Send to the account manager at the body that issued the ISO 9001:2015 certificate (the name is on the certificate; it is not stored in the repo). Many certification bodies keep a public "certified clients" register or a searchable certificate directory with a website field; some also publish case studies.

> **Subject:** Certified-client listing — Global Chemicals LLC, ISO 9001:2015 certificate [number]
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> Global Chemicals LLC holds ISO 9001:2015 certificate [number] issued by [certification body], valid to [date]. Our customers' vendor-qualification teams verify the certificate online, and we would like our entry in your certified-client register (or certificate directory) to show the company website so they land on the right company. There is a name-collision company abroad that is not us.
>
> Entry to show:
> Global Chemicals LLC, Ghala, Muscat, Oman — chemical supply and distribution
> Website: https://gcoman.com
>
> If you publish client case studies, we are happy to contribute one on quality control in chemical supply (batch Certificates of Analysis, SDS management, GCC export documentation).
>
> Kind regards,
> [Name, title]
> Global Chemicals LLC | gcoman.com | +968 9277 2305

**Done when:** the register entry is live with the website link. If the body offers a verification badge with a URL, add the register URL to `sameAs` as well (P5.9).
